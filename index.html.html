📜 The Board Bard Pre-Con Escalation League Rules

1. Buy-in & Prizes

• Buy-in: $6 at the front desk, refunded entirely back in the form of booster packs for prizes.

• Schedule: Play 2 games of Commander per league session (1:00pm – 6:00pm).

• Format: Bring your favorite unaltered Commander Pre-con to play on a level playing field.

2. Pod Pairings (Game 1 & Game 2)

• Game 1: Randomized 4-player pods (or 3/5 depending on attendance).

• Turn Order: Roll 2D6 to determine turn order (highest goes first, clockwise).

• Game 2 ("Winners Pods"): Pair 1st & 2nd place finishers together; pair 3rd & 4th place finishers together.

3. Prize Booster Snake Drafting

• After each game, open booster packs face up on the table (2 packs for a 4-player game, 3 packs for 5 players).

• Pack Chooser: 4th place (first knocked out) picks which prize packs are opened.

• Snake Draft Order: 1st Place picks 1st card ➔ 2nd picks ➔ 3rd picks ➔ 4th picks ➔ 4th picks ➔ 3rd picks ➔ 2nd picks ➔ 1st picks ➔ repeat until all cards are drafted.

4. Deck Building & Upgrades

• Any cards drafted during the league may be added to your precon deck (must remain exactly 100 cards).

• Players are allowed to trade drafted cards with other league members.

• Keep your drafted card pool separate from personal collections.

5. Bi-Yearly League Reset

• On January 1st and July 1st, all decks reset! All drafted cards are removed and decks return to their official stock pre-con lists.
